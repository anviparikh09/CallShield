# CallShield – Project Statement

## Problem Statement

CallShield is designed to help users identify potentially suspicious or scam phone calls. Users may receive calls involving fake KYC requests, repeated calls, suspicious offers, or requests for personal information. It can be difficult to judge the risk of a call immediately.

CallShield analyzes the details entered by the user, calculates a risk score, classifies the call as Low Risk, Suspicious, or High Risk, and stores the generated report in a MySQL database.

## Scope of the Project

The project focuses on analyzing suspicious call characteristics and generating a risk-based report. It includes call data input, risk analysis, risk classification, report generation, and database storage using Java, JDBC, and MySQL.

## Target Users

- General mobile phone users
- Students
- People who frequently receive unknown or suspicious calls
- Users who want to maintain records of suspicious calls

## High-Level Features

- Enter and record call details
- Analyze suspicious call characteristics
- Calculate a risk score
- Classify the call based on risk level
- Generate a scam call report
- Store reports in a MySQL database using JDBC
